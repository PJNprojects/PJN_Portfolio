# Media

Drop photographs and video of the real work here, then reference them from `src/content/site.ts`.

Suggested files (any of these can be missing; the site falls back to the drawings):

- `collar-board.jpg`      — the assembled PCB, top view, on a plain surface
- `collar-case.jpg`       — the printed or cast enclosure, closed
- `collar-open.jpg`       — case open with the board inside
- `collar-on-animal.jpg`  — the collar fitted on a buffalo
- `bench.mp4`             — short clip (≤ 10 s, muted) of testing on the bench, 1920×1080 or smaller

Keep photos under ~400 KB each (export at 1600 px wide, quality 80). Video under 4 MB, H.264, no audio.
